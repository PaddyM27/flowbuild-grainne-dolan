About Us

Clarity, discretion, and care — in every matter.

Grainne Dolan & Co Solicitors is a full-service legal practice built on a straightforward principle: every client deserves the same level of attention, expertise, and honesty, regardless of the complexity of their matter.

We have built our reputation in Ireland over more than 15 years of practise, working with individuals, families, and businesses. As a DSBA member practice, we uphold the highest standards of professional conduct and client confidentiality.
